# Assets

Every model, texture, sound, font and voice in Still Rolling that was not made by hand in the Unreal editor, with the tool or
source that made it and the terms it is used under. Checked against `Content/StillRolling`, `SourceArt/`, the generators in
`Tools/` and every `Docs/agents/*_progress.md` on Nov 13 (AUDIOQA agent); brought up to date on Nov 13 evening by the
INTEGRATE agent when the Freesound recordings, the Gemini voices, the Meshy film frames, photographs and props and the
icon went into the game. No asset store: no Fab, Quixel or Megascans content, and nothing from the We Love Indies library
the owner opened (its free licence asks for a logo intro); Grok was tried for concept images and produced nothing. The
only downloaded content is the Freesound recordings under Creative Commons 0 (section 1).

Under the jam rules AI-generated content made during the contest period counts as our own work; it is listed here anyway
so the submission can say exactly what made what. Section 1 is the only content we did not make.

### Freesound recordings (Creative Commons 0): 118 sound files from 56 recordings (AUDIOLIB agent, Nov 13)

Sourced sounds from Freesound (`freesound.org`), all under **Creative Commons 0** (CC0 1.0 Universal, public domain
dedication, `creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/`). The licence was read on each sound's own page on Nov 13 2026
(the page's licence link points to the CC0 deed and reads "Creative Commons 0"); the searches were filtered to CC0 too.
CC0 asks for no attribution; uploaders are listed because the jam wants every sourced asset listed. All were downloaded
as the uploader's original file from the owner's signed-in account, then cut, filtered, level matched (with the
A-weighted cap) and converted to 48 kHz 16-bit by `Tools/audio/lib_process.py` (recipes: `Tools/audio/lib_recipes.py`).
**In the game since Nov 13 evening** (`Tools/audio/lib_swap.ps1` and the import, INTEGRATE): the 74 files without `New/`
replace synthesised files of the same name (the synthesised versions are kept in `SourceArt/Audio_synth_backup/`, not
shipped; `lib_restore.ps1` puts them back). The 44 files under `New/` are new sounds for the mansion: imported and
cooked (the Audio folder is always cooked). Since Nov 13 night 19 of them are played (MANSION: `fs_carpet_walk_01..06`,
`fs_carpet_sprint_01..05`, `clock_chime_01`, `piano_note_low_01`, `door_distant_01..03`, `amb_corridor_draught_loop`,
`amb_hall_roomtone_loop`, `amb_clock_tick_loop`); the other 25 have no code yet (hooks in `Docs/agents/AUDIOLIB_code_hooks.md`).
Regenerated from `SourceArt/AudioLib/lib_build.json`, so the table holds only sources actually used (56 sources).
**Nobody has listened to them yet.** Report: `Docs/AUDIOLIB_REPORT.md`.
